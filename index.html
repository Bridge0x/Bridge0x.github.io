About

From law to Web3

I'm passionate about innovation, marketing and technology. I studied law at Tor Vergata University in Rome, with a thesis on the Fiat–Chrysler merger, and co-founded my first venture, Roma Poker, while still a student.

I then moved into sports business — marketing projects for Serie A TIM and the Rome launch of Car2Go — and spent the following years in digital marketing and communications across health, sport and corporate companies.

In 2021 I became CMO of Folks Finance, a DeFi protocol on Algorand, and followed the project for over a year. Since 2022 I've coordinated the main projects of Blockchain Italia and run the conference side of Blockchain Forum Italia. In December 2023 I became CEO of TokNox, in 2025 I joined Varla Protocol, and in 2026 I became an Ambassador of Avalanche Team1.

I regularly speak at conferences and universities on marketing, tokenization, DeFi and prediction markets.
